Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Task Manager ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data in an open and transparent manner. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our Outlook add-in.

2. Information We Collect

Our Outlook add-in collects minimal information necessary to provide the service:

  • Email metadata: Email subject and sender information (only when you create a task)
  • User selection: The user you assign tasks to
  • Task data: Title, priority, due date, and notes you provide

3. What We DO NOT Collect

We explicitly DO NOT collect or store:

  • Your email password or credentials
  • Full email content (only excerpts you choose to include in tasks)
  • Email attachments
  • Your contact list
  • Other emails in your mailbox

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Create tasks based on your emails
  • Display task information in your task management dashboard
  • Assign tasks to team members
  • Enable collaboration features

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on our servers with industry-standard encryption. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction.

6. Data Sharing

We DO NOT sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your task data is only visible to members of your team who have access to the task manager.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Export your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time

8. Microsoft Office Integration

This add-in integrates with Microsoft Outlook using the Office Add-ins framework. We only access email data when you explicitly click "Create Task" and only for the specific email you are viewing.

9. Cookies and Tracking

Our add-in does not use cookies for tracking purposes. We may use session storage to maintain your login state during your session.

10. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
Email: support@digitaldream.work
Website: https://task-manager.digitaldream.work

13. GDPR Compliance

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), we comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). You have additional rights under GDPR, including the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

14. Data Retention

We retain your task data for as long as your account is active. When you delete a task, it is permanently removed from our systems. When you delete your account, all associated data is deleted within 30 days.
